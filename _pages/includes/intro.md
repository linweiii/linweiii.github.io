# 🎈 About Me
I am currently a fourth-year Ph.D. student at AI Thrust, Information Hub, [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Li Liu](https://liliu-avril.github.io/) and [Prof. Hui Xiong](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cVDF1tkAAAAJ). Previously, I received my master degree from [City University of Hong Kong](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/), advised by [Prof. Xiangyu Zhao](https://zhaoxyai.github.io/). I am also a research intern at the **WeChat Security Team, Tencent**.

My research focuses on **AI Security and Safety**, with an emphasis on developing robust defense mechanisms and understanding adversarial vulnerabilities in deep learning systems. My recent work spans backdoor defense, large model safety (audio and diffusion models), and red-teaming agents. I have published multiple first-author papers at top-tier venues including **ICML, NeurIPS, AAAI, EMNLP, KDD, and WWW**.

###  Research Interests
- AI Security / Safety
- Red-teaming Agents
- Backdoor Learning
- AIGC Safety
